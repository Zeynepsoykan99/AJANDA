# AJANDA — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2 October 2026

[Türkçe](/AJANDA/privacy/) · [Deutsch](/AJANDA/privacy/de/) · [Español](/AJANDA/privacy/es/) · [Français](/AJANDA/privacy/fr/)

## Summary

AJANDA has no server. You do not need to create an account, the app does not ask you for any identifying information, and it does not store what you write on a server. Your agenda, your diary, your notes, your drawings, your stickers and your recordings stay on your device.

There are **two exceptions** to this, and both exist only to turn what you write into text: **handwriting recognition** and **converting voice notes to text**. Both are described in detail below.

---

## 1. Where your data is kept

Everything you create is stored in your device's own storage:

- Agenda pages, to-do lists, diary and notebook pages
- Your drawings, text boxes, stickers and cover designs
- Your recordings (as files in the app's folder on your device)
- Your theme and language preference, your reminders

We cannot access this data. It is not uploaded to a server, not backed up and not transferred to another device. **If you delete the app from your device, all of this data is deleted.**

---

## 2. Handwriting recognition

So that your handwriting can be turned into searchable text, the **coordinates** of your strokes, the size of the writing area and the language you selected are sent over the internet to **Google's handwriting recognition service** (`inputtools.google.com`), where they are converted to text.

**What is not sent:** your name, any account information, a device identifier or any other identifier. Only the stroke coordinates are sent. The text that comes back is stored **only on your device**.

This conversion works in two ways:

- **Automatically:** in the **My Agenda** and **To-Do** sections, when you write by hand, it runs in the background so that what you have written can be found in search.
- **When you ask for it:** when you select some writing with the lasso tool and choose "convert to text". In the **My Diary** and **My Notes** sections the conversion happens **only this way**, that is, only when you explicitly ask for it — nothing is sent automatically there.

The first time you use this feature, a notice is shown inside the app.

Stroke coordinates are the thing you wrote: put together, your handwriting can be redrawn from them. For this reason, **if there is something you want to keep private, we recommend writing it by hand only with this in mind.**

---

## 3. Converting voice notes to text

So that your voice notes can be turned into searchable text, your recording is passed to the **speech recognition feature provided by your device's operating system**. We do not provide this feature; your device's manufacturer or platform does.

- **On Android devices:** this is handled by the phone's own speech recognition service — on most devices that service belongs to **Google**. Your audio **may be sent over the internet to that service** to be converted to text. That decision is made by the service on your device; the app cannot determine or observe it.
- **On iPhone and iPad:** we request that the conversion be done on the device. However, if your device cannot handle your selected language on its own, your audio **may be sent to Apple's servers.**

**The audio file you record is stored only on your device**; we do not upload it anywhere. Your name, your account and your device information are not sent.

The first time you use this feature, a notice is shown inside the app. To find out how your audio is handled by the operating system, you can also consult your device manufacturer's privacy policy.

---

## 4. The lock (PIN and biometric authentication)

You can lock your diary and your notebooks with a 4-digit PIN, and unlock them with a fingerprint or face recognition if your device supports it.

**What this lock does:** your PIN is stored in your device's hardware-protected secure area (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android); it is **never, under any circumstances, written** to unencrypted general storage. Incorrect PIN attempts are rate-limited; after repeated wrong entries the waiting time increases step by step.

**What this lock does not do — we want to state this plainly:** the lock is an **access gate**. It prevents other people from opening your notebook inside the app, but it **does not encrypt the notebook's contents.** Someone who can reach your device's file system by technical means (for example on a "rooted" or "jailbroken" device, or through a device backup) could read the contents without needing the PIN.

For this reason, the most important protection is **your device's own screen lock and encryption.** We recommend setting a screen lock on your device.

Your biometric data (fingerprint, face) is handled by the operating system; the app does not access or store this data — it only receives the result "authentication succeeded".

---

## 5. Reminders

When you set a reminder on your pages, the notification is scheduled **locally on your device**. No record is made on any server, no remote notification (push) infrastructure is used, and therefore no identifier such as a notification token is created or sent.

---

## 6. Exporting and sharing

When you export a page as a PDF, the file is created on your device and your device's share menu opens. **You** decide where the file goes. The app does not send the file anywhere on its own.

---

## 7. What we do not collect

We want to say this explicitly; the app does **not**:

- Ask you to create an account, and does not collect an e-mail address or phone number
- Collect analytics, usage statistics or crash reports
- Show advertising, or use an advertising identifier or tracking identifier
- Access your location, your contacts, your photos or your calendar
- Sell your data to anyone or share it for marketing purposes

---

## 8. Deleting your data

- You can delete individual pages, notes, recordings or notebooks from inside the app.
- To delete all of your data, it is enough to remove the app from your device.
- Because you have no data on a server, there is no record you would need to ask us to delete.

How long the data sent for handwriting or speech conversion is retained by Google or Apple is subject to those companies' own policies; we have no control over this.

---

## 9. Children's privacy

AJANDA is intended for a **general audience**. It is not designed specifically for children under 13 and is not offered to that age group.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 — the app does not collect account information or personal information from **anyone** in the first place.

If you allow your child to use the app, we recommend helping them use the handwriting and speech conversion features described in sections 2 and 3 with an understanding of what they do.

---

If we update this policy, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top. If there is a significant change, we will announce it inside the app.
